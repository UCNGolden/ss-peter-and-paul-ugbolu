Visitor Etiquette & Expectations for Catholic Worship

  • Liturgical Reverence: Please genuflect or bow toward the Blessed Sacrament in the Tabernacle when entering and leaving your pew.
  • Holy Silence: Maintain quiet reflection before Mass to foster prayer and recollection for yourself and fellow worshippers.
  • Modest Attire: Reverent and decent clothing is requested for all worship services out of respect for God's house.
  • Mobile Phones: Kindly switch off your cellular phones or place them on total silence throughout the duration of Holy Mass.
  • Reception of Holy Communion: Practicing Catholics in the state of grace are invited to receive Holy Communion. Non-Catholics and visitors are warmly encouraged to come forward with arms crossed over the chest to receive a priestly blessing.
