The Chaplet of the Divine Mercy

Given by Our Lord Jesus Christ to Saint Faustina Kowalska in 1935. Especially recited at the 3:00 PM Hour of Great Mercy.

"At three o'clock, implore My mercy, especially for sinners; and, if only for a brief moment, immerse yourself in My Passion... This is the hour of great mercy for the whole world." (Diary of St. Faustina, 1320)

How to Recite the Chaplet (Using Ordinary Rosary Beads)

  1. Make the Sign of the Cross.
  2. Recite 1 Our Father, 1 Hail Mary, and The Apostles' Creed.
  3. On the large bead before each decade, pray:
    "Eternal Father, I offer You the Body and Blood, Soul and Divinity of Your dearly beloved Son, Our Lord Jesus Christ, in atonement for our sins and those of the whole world."
  4. On the 10 small beads of each decade, pray:
    "For the sake of His sorrowful Passion, have mercy on us and on the whole world."
  5. After five decades, conclude by chanting three times:
    "Holy God, Holy Mighty One, Holy Immortal One, have mercy on us and on the whole world."
  6. Closing Prayer:
    "Eternal God, in whom mercy is endless and the treasury of compassion inexhaustible, look kindly upon us and increase Your mercy in us, that in difficult moments we might not despair nor become despondent, but with great confidence submit ourselves to Your holy will, which is Love and Mercy itself. Amen."

Examination of Conscience for Confession

A prayerful guide based on the Ten Commandments and Precepts of the Church to prepare worthy confession.

1. My Relationship with God

  • Have I doubted, denied, or compromised my Catholic faith?
  • Have I engaged in idolatry, superstition, occult practices, or consulting fortune-tellers?
  • Have I taken the Holy Name of God in vain or used foul language?
  • Have I deliberately missed Sunday Mass or Holy Days of Obligation without grave medical reason?
  • Have I received Holy Communion while conscious of unconfessed mortal sin?

2. Charity Toward Family & Neighbour

  • Have I honoured, respected, and supported my parents and family elders?
  • Have I harboured hatred, malice, grudges, or refused to forgive others?
  • Have I engaged in physical violence, abortion, or encouraged any act against human life?
  • Have I committed sexual sins (fornication, adultery, pornography, or impure thoughts)?
  • Have I stolen, cheated in business transactions, taken bribes, or refused to pay just debts?
  • Have I told lies, slandered others, or destroyed someone's good name through gossip?
The Act of Contrition:

"O my God, I am heartily sorry for having offended Thee, and I detest all my sins because of Thy just punishments, but most of all because they offend Thee, my God, Who art all-good and deserving of all my love. I firmly resolve, with the help of Thy grace, to sin no more, and to avoid the near occasions of sin. Amen."
